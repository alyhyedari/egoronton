A little context.

EGOR World is a community tribute inspired by Egor Zhgun’s art. This page brings together the source links behind our sticker references and explains what those links do—and do not—establish.

Not an official artist page. EGOR World and the $EGOR community token are not presented here as projects owned, approved or endorsed by Egor Zhgun. A reference to an artist or a sticker is not evidence of token involvement.

Portfolio reference

Egor Zhgun and Utya

The Behance project “Utya Duck Telegram Stickers” is attributed to Egor Zhgun in the search results reviewed for this release. It is the portfolio reference behind this page’s Utya credit.

The separate UtyaDuck directory listing is a way to find the sticker pack, not proof of ownership of a token or a license to reuse every image.

Evidence limit: the reviewed attribution comes from indexed Behance project results, not an independent audit of every sticker, its original creation or its rights. Visit the linked project for its current presentation and credits.

Sticker reference, not an authorship claim

Resistance Dog

The ResistanceDog directory page identifies an animated sticker set. The page text retrieved for this review did not identify its artist.

That listing alone does not establish who created the original character, who animated a particular sticker, or who founded or owns a token. We therefore do not use it to attribute Resistance Dog or the REDO token to Egor Zhgun.

The REDO room in EGOR World is a community interpretation. Its themes and captions are editorial choices, not statements of an artist’s intent or endorsement by Telegram, Pavel Durov or a token project.

Keep the credit clear

Art, stickers and tokens are different things.

  • A portfolio credit supports the identified project; it does not automatically extend to all related characters, sticker sets or tokens.
  • A public sticker listing is not a commercial art license. Credits do not replace permission.
  • The $EGOR community’s use of a name or artwork does not establish artist affiliation, ownership or endorsement.
  • Historical transfer comments do not verify a recipient’s identity or prove an artist’s participation. See the reward-record limitations.
