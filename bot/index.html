A FEW CLEAR ANSWERS

Before you say hello

Can anyone chat with it privately?

The current private experience is the website welcome and group portal. Private management is owner-only. Public /egor and optional model-backed conversation happen in enabled groups

Does the AI manage or approve members?

No. Generative replies are separate from administrative commands and portal checks. A model response cannot grant permissions, approve entry or execute moderation

Does visiting this page link my Telegram account?

No. Links open Telegram. This page does not load a Telegram SDK, read your Telegram profile or expose the bot’s private database

Is the game connected to the bot?

Game-account synchronization is a separate service and is not activated by these links. This release connects the website, bot welcome and community entrance

What comes next?

The direction is a richer EGOR community: more useful replies, more stories and clearer tools for hosts. Future integrations will be labelled when they are ready; this page does not promise unreleased features
