EGOR field notes / version 0.4.6

A little plan.
Clear boundaries.

This is a proposed twelve-month sequence for the independent EGOR community project. It is not a dated delivery commitment, an investment forecast or an announcement of a partnership.

How to read the map: “Built” means a feature is included in this site. “Proposed” means work that still needs team approval, time and resources. “Outside decisions” means an outcome controlled by another party. Selecting a chapter does not complete a milestone.

Starting point / built into this site

Come on in

  • The interactive light room has day settings, motion controls and optional sound.
  • Sticker stories, Utya and the REDO room remain part of the world.
  • Portrait Studio creates images in the browser without connecting a wallet.

These are local site features, not proof of token utility, platform approval or a successful production deployment. Artist credits do not imply endorsement or a commercial art license.

Proposed / months 1–2 from team approval

Make it clear

  • Compare the project name, contract address and official links across public profiles.
  • Collect source links for fees, migration and liquidity. Record unknowns instead of filling gaps with assumptions.
  • Maintain a public checklist of corrections, dated evidence and unresolved questions.

Proposed finish line: one linked checklist identifying each source, what it supports and what it does not. A submitted correction is not a confirmed platform update.

Proposed / months 3–5

Make it together

  • Try small community art prompts with clear participation terms.
  • Gather feedback on the studio and prioritize useful improvements.
  • Publish a small showcase only with contributors’ permission and credits.
  • Test any studio update on narrow screens, keyboard navigation and reduced-motion settings.

Proposed finish line: a credited showcase and a tested studio update. No prizes, payments, audience targets or participation levels are promised.

Outside decisions / proposed months 6–9

Open a few doors

  • Request current written Topblast terms and a project-specific eligibility answer.
  • Prepare applications only where requirements can be checked.
  • Track submitted, pending, approved and rejected responses separately.

Proposed finish line: a public status log with supporting links. Listings, promotions, response times and market conditions are not controlled by EGOR. An application is not an approval.

Proposed / months 10–12

Keep the good bits

  • Review published features and community feedback.
  • Explain what shipped, slipped or stopped, with links to the actual work.
  • Choose a focused next plan based on capacity and evidence.

Proposed finish line: a public review, not a promised price, market capitalization, audience size or exchange rank. The sequence can change; material changes should be disclosed.

Separate platform / unverified current eligibility

Where Topblast fits

Topblast is an external platform. Its milestones and benefits must not be treated as promises made by EGOR. Current rules and EGOR’s eligibility have not been independently verified for this roadmap.

The attempted page review was blocked by HTTP 403. Earlier extracts or screenshots are not a substitute for accessible, current terms and a project-specific written response. This release therefore does not publish benefit thresholds as established facts.

Questions to resolve before announcing any benefit

  1. What are the current eligibility thresholds, measurement methods and deadlines?
  2. Do the rules apply to this specific EGOR contract and its present status?
  3. Which benefits are discretionary, application-based, paid or dependent on a third party?
  4. Can a current public source and written confirmation be linked?

Sources and limits

  • Historical reward records: nine previously reviewed records are presented with limitations. Transfer comments do not establish a fee percentage, recipient identity, future payment or artist endorsement.
  • Token details: project-supplied metadata and public verification links. Check the deployed contract and live service before any transaction.
  • Release metadata: describes this static package; it is not evidence that the live site has been updated.
  • Project X and Telegram portal: project communication channels, not independent confirmation of external claims.

No listing, payment, guaranteed growth, artist endorsement or commercial art license is promised by this roadmap. The site remains a browser-based creative experience without wallet connection.
