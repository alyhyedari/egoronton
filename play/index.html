Controls, saving & the prototype

WASD to move; drag the view to look. Arrow keys turn and move. On a phone, use the left stick and drag the right side to look. Shift to run, E to interact nearby, J for your notebook, Escape to pause. Movement works with Persian and English keyboard layouts.

Progress saves only in this browser, on this site address. Clearing browser data erases it. Start again resets this neighborhood after confirmation. No wallet or purchase.

Independent community prototype. Original procedural scenery; no artist endorsement. This is not the finished game.
