01 / MAKE IT YOURS

Think in lines.

Drag to pan. Scroll over the focused chart to zoom. Choose a tool, then drag to draw. Levels need one click. Your drawings keep their time and price coordinates when you zoom.

02 / KNOW YOUR DATA

No pretend live.

This workspace uses deterministic synthetic datasets for each quote. They are not cross-rates or real trades. Volume is example EGOR units. Higher intervals aggregate hourly examples. EMA is seeded from the first close; SMA needs a full period. RSI uses Wilder smoothing; MACD uses 12/26 EMA and a 9-period signal.

03 / STAY IN CONTROL

Room to explore.

No external chart embed, wallet access or background polling. Images exported here include the demonstration label. Saved drawings are separate for each pair and interval. Chart preferences stay in this browser. This is a custom chart, not TradingView software. Live analysis requires verified token identities and an historical market feed.
