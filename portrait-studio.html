The short version

Small tools. Big character.

  1. Build your scene. Add a photo, a poseable EGOR, original flat artwork or text. The top layer is in front. Use the arrows to reorder.
  2. Find your angle. Move, resize, rotate and flip a layer. Pose mode exposes joint handles; the body-part menu offers keyboard-accessible angle, length and visibility controls.
  3. Make room. Erase a selected layer to put EGOR behind a shoulder or remove a detail. Restore is non-destructive. Locked layers cannot be edited.
  4. Take it with you. Export a square or circular PNG. Save a project to continue later, including your photos, layers, masks and rig settings.

Canvas focused: arrow keys nudge; Shift + arrows moves 10 px. Delete removes a layer. Escape cancels a drag or brush stroke.

Touch: drag with one finger. Scroll the page outside the canvas; when zoomed, use the canvas scrollbars. No automatic background removal, generative fill or 3D posing. Up to 12 layers and 40 undo steps. Photos are resized to at most 1536 px on import. Work is held in memory, not autosaved. Save your project before leaving.
