The Guaranteed Moon

Six decisions, five endings; no purchases, wallets, external links or transactions.

Only Persian and English are translated. Switching language does not change your route; the language preference is not saved.

Ink Credits are fictional and have no monetary value: they cannot be bought, sold, transferred, withdrawn or converted. This story makes no accusations against any real project.

Privacy, saving and prototype limits

This page makes no internet requests. Choices stay in memory until you select Save. Manual saves use this prototype’s own browser storage key; local-file or private browsing modes may refuse storage. Starting again does not delete a manual save. Rewinding also removes subsequent consequences and does not stack rewards on the previous balance.

Manual save tampering cannot be prevented; this prototype is not a secure account or an economic system. In the story, the currency is called Gram and the network TON; this naming makes no historical or technical claim about the currency’s official name.
